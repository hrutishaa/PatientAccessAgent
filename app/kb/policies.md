## Clinic hours
Synthetic Health clinics are open Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM, and Saturday 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Clinics are closed on Sundays and major holidays.

## Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows may be noted on your record after two occurrences in 12 months.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your visit so other patients are seen on time.

## What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any referral paperwork. Arrive 15 minutes early for new patient visits.

## Insurance
We accept most major insurance plans, including Medicare and Medicaid. Coverage questions about a specific plan are handled by our billing office at the number on your statement.

## Parking
Free patient parking is available in the garage attached to the main building. Bring your parking ticket to the front desk for validation.

## Telehealth
Many follow-up visits can be done by video through the patient portal. You need a device with a camera and a stable internet connection.

## Patient portal
The patient portal (MyChart-style) lets you view results, message your care team, pay bills, and manage appointments. Sign up with the activation code on your visit summary.

## Billing
Questions about a bill or payment plan go to the billing office. Payment plans are available for balances over 200 dollars.
